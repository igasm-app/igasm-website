Support

igasm, by Venex Labs. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Write to us

Email admin@igasm.in. A person reads every message and replies, usually within two working days. Please never send us what you logged: we do not need it to help, and email is not the private place your journal is.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, igasm. On Android: Google Play, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, igasm. Everything you logged stays yours and stays free to use after a plan ends.

I paid but premium is not showing

Open the plans screen in igasm and tap Restore an earlier purchase. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase.

I have a new phone

Your account lives on this phone for now. Moving it to a new phone with an email sign-in is coming soon; until then, use Export everything in Settings before you switch so you keep a full copy.

How do I end a partner link or leave a room?

In Settings, open Partners and sharing and tap Unlink. In a room, tap Step out of this room. Either takes a moment and needs no reason, and your own moments stay with you.

I forgot my app lock PIN

The app lock lives only on your phone, so we cannot see or reset it. On the lock screen tap Forgot PIN?: Face ID or Touch ID opens it if this phone has it, and otherwise igasm shows you the honest way back in.

How do I export or delete everything?

Settings, then Export everything for a copy, or Erase everything to delete your account and all of it for good. More on the delete page.
