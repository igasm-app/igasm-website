Perimenopause and a changing rhythm: dryness, desire, and bleeding that deserves a check.

Written by the igasm team from the studies and health services cited on this page. Not yet reviewed by a clinician: a named reviewer and a review date will appear here before this page is listed in search.

Dryness is common now

Dryness and pain with sex become common around menopause. Some people find a lubricant during sex, or a moisturizer made for intimate use, makes sex more comfortable, the Menopause Society notes. A clinician can talk through other options.

Strong evidence · Source: Menopause Society 2020

Desire is more than hormones

In a large study following people through menopause, desire dipped on average, but overall health, mood, and how much sex mattered to them shaped sex life more than menopause itself.

Strong evidence · Source: SWAN, Avis et al. 2009

Unusual bleeding deserves a check

Periods often get irregular now, which is expected. Bleeding after sex, very heavy bleeding, or any bleeding after your periods have stopped for good is worth getting checked by a clinician.

Strong evidence · Source: NHS
