How these are written

  • Each card is short, says what the evidence is (strong, moderate or mixed) and links the study or health service behind it.
  • Cards describe people in general and invite you to notice your own experience. Nothing here names a condition, predicts anything, or is a reason to start or stop any treatment.

Pregnancy is possible at any point in the cycle, including during a period. igasm is not a method of contraception or a conception aid and cannot tell you when pregnancy is or is not possible. For contraception or pregnancy planning, talk to a clinician.
