Reading it

Only about 13% of tracked cycles run exactly 28 days, and the average across more than 600,000 cycles was about 29 (Bull et al. 2019). For adults, the international gynaecology federation describes cycles of 24 to 38 days as within the usual range, with the shortest and longest usually within 7 to 9 days of each other (FIGO, Munro et al. 2018). These are descriptions of people in general, not a test. If your cycles often fall outside them, change a lot, or something else worries you, it is worth mentioning to a clinician; the appointment checklist can help.

Pregnancy is possible at any point in the cycle, including during a period. igasm is not a method of contraception or a conception aid and cannot tell you when pregnancy is or is not possible. For contraception or pregnancy planning, talk to a clinician.
