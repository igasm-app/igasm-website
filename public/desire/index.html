Desire does not always come first

For many people desire shows up once things begin rather than before, and most people experience both kinds at different times. Researchers describe this as responsive desire; it is a common way desire works, and it can change with stress, seasons of life and relationships.

Source: Basson model, reviewed in Brotto and Graham (2021).
