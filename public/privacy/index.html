Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. Applies to the igasm app (iOS and Android) and igasm.in.

igasm has not launched yet. This policy describes exactly how the app handles data today. It will be reviewed by privacy counsel before launch, and any material change will be shown in the app before it applies to you.

The short version

What we keep

The moments, body and cycle entries, notes and settings you choose to log, encrypted in transit and at rest on our database provider's servers, so a lost or replaced phone never loses your history. Our servers can read this data, because that is how your patterns are calculated and how a partner you choose can see what you share with them. We say this plainly rather than claim otherwise.

What we never do

Sell your data. Use it for advertising. Put advertising or third-party analytics tools in the app. Share it with anyone you did not choose. Make any medical, fertility or contraception claim from it.

What you can always do

See, export or delete everything from inside the app, lock the app with a PIN or Face ID, withdraw your consent, and unlink a partner at any time.

1. Who we are

igasm is published by Venex Labs. For anything about this policy or your data, write to admin@igasm.in. Our EU representative and the full legal details of the controller will be listed here before launch in the EU.

2. Who igasm is for

igasm is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it and everything in it.

3. What we collect

  • What you log. Intimate moments and closeness, ratings, dials, activities, notes, cycle and period entries, symptoms, daily check-ins and any hormone lab values you enter. All of it is optional. Under the EU GDPR this is special-category data about your health and sex life, and we process it only with your explicit consent.
  • People you name.If you choose to answer the optional question of how you describe yourself (for example woman, man or non-binary, or your own word), we keep that answer privately on your account. Only you can read it. It is used for one thing: to suggest, in the app, whether period features might apply to you, which you can always change. We never ask about or store your sexual orientation, and you can change or remove this answer in About you at any time. If you add a nickname for someone you were with, that label is stored privately with your moment and shown only to you. We never import contacts.
  • Partner links. If you link with a partner by a single-use code, we store that link, what you chose to share with them, and their consent.
  • Account and app data. An anonymous account identifier (you do not need an email to start), your settings, and, if you subscribe, your subscription status from the app store.
  • What we do not collect. No location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising identifiers, no analytics or advertising tracking.

4. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To store your history and show it back to you, and to calculate your own patterns: your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), given in the app before anything is saved, and our contract with you.
  • To share specific moments with a partner you linked: your separate consent, and theirs. Either of you can end it at any time.
  • Optional AI-written insights, if offered: only with a separate consent you can decline, and only through a provider bound not to train on or retain your data. This feature is off unless you turn it on.
  • To keep the service secure and to meet legal obligations: our legitimate interests and legal duties.

5. Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, each under a contract that limits them to running igasm for us: our database and hosting provider (Supabase), Apple and Google for purchases, and our subscription manager (RevenueCat), which receives only your account identifier and subscription status, never what you log; and, only if you turn on AI insights, an AI provider under a no-training, no-retention agreement. We list each provider by name in the app before launch. We never sell or rent data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

6. How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete something it leaves your timeline at once and is permanently removed from our systems within 45 days, and from backups on their normal rotation (a daily job erases deleted entries 30 days after you delete them). When you delete your account, everything about you is erased at once, including the record of the consent you gave. The same applies when you ask us to delete it by email; see igasm.in/delete.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can access, export, correct and delete your data, and withdraw consent, from inside the app or by writing to us. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you also have the rights to restriction, objection and portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. US residents, including in Washington, Connecticut and Nevada, have the consumer health data rights their laws give them; we honor them for everyone. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, row-level access rules that let only you (and a partner you chose, for what you shared) read your entries, an optional app lock, and content-free notifications. No system is perfect; if a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Law enforcement and legal requests

Because our servers can read your data, we could be compelled by valid legal process to produce it. We will challenge requests that are overbroad, tell you when we lawfully can, and we keep as little as possible: for example, igasm never stores a pregnancy status. Deleting your data removes it from what could ever be produced.

10. Not medical advice

igasm is an informational wellness journal. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is never a contraceptive or fertility tool. Talk to a clinician about your health.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will show you in the app before the change applies, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

12. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section is our consumer health data privacy policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in Nevada, Connecticut and other states. We apply it to everyone, wherever you live.

  • Consumer health data we collect. Intimate moments and closeness entries, ratings and dials, how a moment felt, comfort and discomfort, cycle and period entries, symptoms, life stage and seasons you choose, how you describe yourself if you choose to say, daily check-ins (desire, energy, stress, sleep), and hormone lab values you type in.
  • Where it comes from. Only from you, typed into the app. If a partner you linked shares a moment with you, that shared moment comes from them, with their consent. We never buy data or collect it from other sources.
  • Why we collect and use it. To keep your history, to show your own patterns back to you, to share what you choose with a partner you linked, and, only if you turn it on, to write AI insights. Never for advertising, never to build a profile for anyone else.
  • Who we share it with. Only the service providers in section 5, acting for us under contract, and a partner you linked, for what you chose to share. We do not sell consumer health data, and we have no affiliates that receive it.
  • Your rights. You can confirm what we hold and see it (Settings, then Export everything), export it, delete it, and withdraw consent, all inside the app with no request needed. You can also write to admin@igasm.in. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we respond to an appeal within 45 days; if you still disagree, you can contact your state attorney general.

13. This website

igasm.in has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, which part of the page you joined from and the page that linked you here, in our database (Supabase), only to tell you when igasm launches. Write to admin@igasm.in and we remove it. The site is hosted by Vercel and its fonts are served by Google Fonts; like any web server, they receive your IP address and browser details to deliver the page. The invite page (igasm.in/c) never sends the code in its link to any server.
