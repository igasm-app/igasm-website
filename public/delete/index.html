Delete your account

igasm, by Venex Labs. Last updated 1 November 2026.

In the app (fastest)

Open igasm, go to Settings, tap Erase everything, then Erase. Your account and everything in it are erased from our servers at once, and the app forgets them on that phone. If you want a copy first, tap Export everything on the same screen.

Without the app

If you added an email to your account, write to admin@igasm.in from that address with the subject "Delete my igasm account". We reply to that address to confirm before anything is erased, then erase the account and tell you when it is done, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

If you never added an email, the account is not linked to anything that identifies you, so we cannot find it from outside the app. Signing in on your phone and using Erase everything is the way to delete it.

What is deleted

  • Every moment you logged, with its details, notes and your side of any shared moment.
  • Your cycle, period and symptom entries, daily check-ins, life stages and seasons.
  • Your partner links, invites and the record of the consent you gave.
  • Your profile, settings and subscription status kept by igasm.

A partner keeps their own moments. Their side of a moment you logged goes with that moment, because the moment was yours. A subscription is billed by Apple or Google, so cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings as well; deleting the account does not cancel billing on its own.

What is kept

Nothing about you. If our hosting provider holds encrypted database backups, they roll over on their normal schedule, after which no copy remains.
